About EyeLabs AI

Clinical expertise meets machine learning.

EyeLabs AI builds software for eye care — diagnostics, clinical decision support, and research tools. Everything we build is grounded in real ophthalmic practice and reviewed by practicing specialists.

Gurpal Virdi, MD MA

Founder & CEO

Gurpal Virdi is the founder and CEO of EyeLabs AI. He completed his undergraduate studies at Washington University in St. Louis and earned his medical degree from the University of Missouri, where he trained in ophthalmology. He is currently completing a refractive surgery fellowship at Parkhurst NuVision under Dr. Gregory Parkhurst — principal investigator of the FDA EVO ICL trial and one of the country's highest-volume EVO ICL surgeons.

As a refractive and cataract surgeon, he offers the full spectrum of vision correction — EVO ICL, LASIK, PRK, SMILE, refractive lens exchange, and cataract surgery — with a high volume of cataract surgery, including astigmatism correction with toric IOLs.
